# Brag Plan: PNP Internship Attendance System (P-IDTMS)

## 1. Planning Rubric Answers

1. **Product Name**: PNP Internship Attendance System (P-IDTMS — Personnel Information and Daily Time Management System)
2. **What It Is**: Centralized institutional attendance, daily time tracking, biometric verification, and document management system built for PNP ITMS interns.
3. **Core Features Highlighted**:
   - Secure PWA Initialization and Sign-In Access with Terms & Conditions compliance
   - Real-Time Intern Dashboard with live progress, rendered hours (0h / 300h required), and biometric enrollment prompts
   - Two-Factor Biometric Attendance Pipeline: QR verification followed by face authentication and instant "Time Out Recorded / Done for Today" completion
   - Automated Civil Service Daily Time Record (DTR) calculation with A4 PDF export, PNG export, and printing
   - Centralized OJT Document Vault with approved attachments and supervisor verification
4. **Target Audience**: PNP ITMS Leadership, Internship Coordinators, Academic Supervisors, Capstone Defense Panel, and Interns.
5. **Tone**: Institutional Enterprise Technology, Cinematic 3D Product Presentation, Clean Government Software Aesthetic.
6. **Aspect Ratio & Length**: 16:9 Landscape (1920x1080), exactly 60.00 seconds.
7. **Music & Audio**: Professional technological electronic beat (`bgm.mp3`), synced with Kokoro TTS narration (`af_heart`).
8. **Real Media Inventory**:
   - `01_splash_loader.png` — Splash / initialization screen with PNP-IDTMS seal
   - `02_sign_in.png` — Official credentials login screen
   - `03_terms_legal.png` — Terms and conditions acceptance flow
   - `04_intern_dashboard.png` — Intern dashboard with progress and hours
   - `05_attendance_verification_gate.png` — Attendance verification step (QR & camera gate)
   - `06_attendance_completed.png` — Verified time-out attendance completed status
