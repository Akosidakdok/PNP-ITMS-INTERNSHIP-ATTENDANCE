# Composition Brief: PNP Internship Attendance System

## System Specifications
- **Dimensions**: 1920x1080 (16:9 Landscape)
- **Frame Rate**: 30 FPS
- **Duration**: Exactly 60.00 seconds (1800 frames)
- **Color Palette**:
  - Deep Navy Base: `#050a18`, `#0a142e`
  - Institutional PNP Blue: `#0047AB`, `#1d4ed8`
  - Gold Accent: `#eab308`, `#facc15`
  - Crisp Cyan Highlight: `#38bdf8`, `#06b6d4`
  - High-Contrast White: `#ffffff`, `#f8fafc`
- **Typography**: Inter / system-ui, bold sans-serif headers, clean technical subtitles.

## Scene Layout & 3D Motion
1. **Scene 1 (0.0s – 6.0s)**: Opening logo reveal, glowing tech grid, title reveal with light sweep.
2. **Scene 2 (6.0s – 14.0s)**: 3D phone entrance showing splash loader, rotating smoothly to login and terms screens.
3. **Scene 3 (14.0s – 24.0s)**: Intern dashboard hero shot in 3D frame, camera dolly-in, highlighting hours rendered and biometric banner.
4. **Scene 4 (24.0s – 36.0s)**: Dual-phone attendance sequence showing 3-step verification flow and verified completion status with scanning effect.
5. **Scene 5 (36.0s – 46.0s)**: Daily Time Record (DTR) screen, rotating into frontal view with emphasis on 80h total and PDF export.
6. **Scene 6 (46.0s – 53.0s)**: My Documents vault with approved attachment card and upload button.
7. **Scene 7 (53.0s – 60.0s)**: Camera pulls back to reveal complete multi-screen 3D wall with all 8 real screens floating at staggered depths, closing on PNP & ITMS insignias.
